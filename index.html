Who We Are
Built by Operators, Backed by Experience

One Matchmaking is a partnership platform purpose-built for the matchmaking industry. We believe the best matchmakers in America are independent operators - professionals who've built real businesses on trust, intuition, and relationships.

We're not here to replace what works. We're here to give proven matchmakers the operational support, shared resources, and national network they need to reach more clients while staying true to their craft.

Backed by seasoned investors with decades of experience in consumer brands, M&A advisory, and scaling service businesses, One Matchmaking brings institutional resources without institutional rigidity.

The Partnership
What You Gain. What You Keep.
We're not buying your business to change it. We're joining forces so you can focus on what you do best - matching people - while we handle the rest.

Keep Your Brand

Your name, your reputation, your client relationships. We invest in what you've built - we don't erase it. Your brand stays front and center in your market.

Immediate Liquidity

Take meaningful capital off the table today while retaining equity upside. Our partnership structures are designed for a second bite of the apple when the platform scales.

National Network

Access a coast-to-coast referral network of elite matchmakers. Cross-market introductions mean more matches, happier clients, and expanded reach beyond your geography.

Technology & Marketing

From CRM systems to digital marketing and lead generation - we provide the operational infrastructure that independent firms rarely have the resources to build alone.

Operational Support

HR, accounting, compliance, legal - we handle the back office so you can spend your time on clients, not paperwork. Run your practice, not your admin.

Growth Capital

Ready to hire, expand to a new city, or launch a premium tier? Our platform provides the growth capital and strategic guidance to take your practice to the next level.
